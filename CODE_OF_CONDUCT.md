# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.
Contributors should be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Unacceptable behaviour includes
harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information.

Report conduct concerns privately to the Olano maintainers. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behaviour is harmful.

This short policy is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
