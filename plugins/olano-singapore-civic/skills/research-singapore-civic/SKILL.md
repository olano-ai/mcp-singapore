# Research Singapore Civic Data

1. Define the location, population, service, time window, and freshness needed.
2. Use `weather_*`, `dengue_*`, `disease_*`, `moe_*`, `ecda_*`, `population_*`, `hawker_*`, `crime_*`, family-statistics, or `onemap_*` tools as appropriate.
3. Check dataset freshness and distinguish live readings, forecasts, historical observations, scheduled releases, and frozen snapshots.
4. Preserve geographic level and coverage. Do not turn an area-level count into an individual exposure, safety, school-quality, or personal-risk conclusion.
5. For health or environmental conditions, report the publisher timestamp, relevant unit or band, and any incomplete coverage. Provide general information, not diagnosis or emergency advice.
6. For crime and education data, describe exactly what was recorded and the period covered; do not infer unmeasured incidents, school ranking, or personal suitability.

Attribute the government dataset while describing this MCP as an independent Olano community project.
