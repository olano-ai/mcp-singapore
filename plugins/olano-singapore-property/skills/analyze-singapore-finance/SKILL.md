# Analyze Singapore Finance

1. Gather the amount, tenure, rate type, user-supplied rate assumptions, income assumptions, and comparison period.
2. Use `finance_mortgage_rates_latest` or `finance_mortgage_rates_history` for official reference-rate context. A reference rate is not a lender offer.
3. Use `finance_mortgage_payment`, `finance_mortgage_stress_test`, and `finance_mortgage_affordability` for transparent calculations. Report formulas, assumptions, units, and rounding.
4. Add `mas_fx_*`, `cpi_*`, `median_income_*`, `singstat_household_income_*`, `singstat_wages_*`, `hdb_resale_*`, or `ura_private_property_*` only when relevant and period-aligned.
5. Do not imply live SGX coverage, insurance premiums, product eligibility, approval, or personalised recommendations. Use a configured third-party market source only when explicitly identified and requested.
6. Label observations, user inputs, calculations, and interpretations separately. Check current official rules before discussing regulatory limits.

This workflow provides factual and educational scenarios, not investment, insurance, mortgage, tax, or legal advice.
